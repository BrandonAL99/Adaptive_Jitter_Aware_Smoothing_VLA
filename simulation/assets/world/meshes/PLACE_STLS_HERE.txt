Place environment STL mesh files here as you create them.

Planned meshes for the physical setup:
  table.stl           <- black desk surface
  desk_mat.stl        <- grey circle mat on top of desk
  box_left.stl        <- cardboard box (left, for blue cube in sorting)
  box_right.stl       <- cardboard box (right, for red cube in sorting)
  cube_red.stl        <- red cube (~3cm sides)
  cube_blue.stl       <- blue cube (~3cm sides)

Once STLs are ready:
  1. Add <mesh> entries in mjcf/world_base.xml under <asset>
  2. Replace the placeholder box/cylinder geoms in the scene XMLs
     with <geom type="mesh" mesh="your_mesh_name"/>

Notes:
  - Export from CAD/Blender in meters (MuJoCo uses SI units)
  - Origin should be at the geometric centre or bottom face as appropriate
  - Simplify mesh polygon count for simulation performance
